# Firmcraft — Founder Video: Script and Filming Guide

**Purpose:** a 60–90 second video for the About page, beside the bio.
**Tone:** calm, direct, professional, as if speaking to a prospective client across a table. Not a pitch.

---

## Script (about 190 words, roughly 80 seconds)

Speak it in your own words; it does not need to be word for word. Each paragraph can be filmed as a separate take.

> **1.** I'm Doyle Dettro, and I founded Firmcraft.
>
> **2.** For more than twenty years I've worked on the systems businesses run on: designing processes, architecting solutions, and implementing them for organizations around the world. I trained in accounting and began my career at Arthur Andersen, so I look at technology the way an owner or a chief financial officer does. What will it cost, and what will it return?
>
> **3.** Artificial intelligence is arriving in most organizations without that discipline. People adopt individual tools, subscriptions accumulate, and no one owns the result.
>
> **4.** Firmcraft exists to change that. We advise on where artificial intelligence will produce measurable value, we implement what that requires, and we operate it on an ongoing basis, with your data kept under your control.
>
> **5.** I lead every engagement myself, whether I'm working with the owner of a growing business or with an executive team.
>
> **6.** If you're weighing what artificial intelligence should mean for your organization, I'd welcome the conversation.

*All facts are from your confirmed bio. Nothing names an employer other than Arthur Andersen, and nothing implies a current role elsewhere.*

---

## Filming guide (phone is fine)

**Camera**
- Use the rear camera, held **horizontally**, at 4K or 1080p and 30 fps.
- Put it on a tripod or a stack of books at **eye level**, about 1–1.5 m away. Frame from mid-chest up, with a little space above your head.
- Look into the lens, not at the screen. A teleprompter app on a second device placed just under the lens helps.

**Light**
- Face a window, with soft daylight on your face. Never sit with a window behind you.
- Turn off overhead lights if they cast a different colour.

**Sound** (matters more than picture)
- A small wired lavalier mic plugged into the phone, clipped mid-chest. Inexpensive ones work well.
- Use a quiet room with soft furnishings, and turn off fans and notifications.

**Setting and wardrobe**
- An office or study background with some depth, a few feet behind you, uncluttered. No logos, screens, or anything identifying another company.
- Wear a solid dark jacket or shirt. Avoid fine stripes, small checks, and bright white.

**Takes**
- Record each numbered paragraph as its own clip, 2–3 takes each.
- Pause for 2 seconds before and after speaking.
- Then record 20 seconds of you silently at the desk (reading, writing) for cutaways.

**Hand-off**
- Put the raw files in a folder called `founder-video` in the firmcraft-site project folder on your Mac, then let Claude or Claudia know. Raw files are not committed to the repo.
- Editing (cutting takes together, colour, sound levels, burned-in or caption-track subtitles, export) is handled for you. You'll see the cut before it goes live.
